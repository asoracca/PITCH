      How to play

      1. Choose Quick game, or select a role and format.
      2. Wait for two contestants and a judge. Public topics and sides are random.
      3. Write an opening, a rebuttal, and a closing. Each round allows one argument of up to 600 characters.
      4. The judge scores reasoning, rebuttal and clarity after all three rounds.
      5. Contestants gain or lose Beef Rating. The judge earns a priority contestant ticket.

      Draws count toward your five placement games. Empty games earn no points or tickets.

      This skeleton uses text and human judges. Voice effects and AI practice are not enabled.

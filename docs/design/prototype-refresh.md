# PITCH prototype refresh

The Home page leads with a concise practice-and-confidence pitch and uses the same shared eight-tab sidebar as every other page, including the same mobile menu and account controls. A smaller transparent cutout of the studio sits directly on the navy background with no image panel or clipped frame. Flat illustrated students walk into it and emerge in full professional outfits: blazers, collared tops, tailored trousers and formal shoes. The change happens inside the pod. The outgoing runway curves downward to a visible end platform; the walking path stays on the artwork and fades out before the end, including in the reduced-motion arrangement. The old progress/narration bar is removed; “How it works” remains a link beside the practice button.

The original scene was inspired by the composition of [Alex Bender’s Etail landing page](https://dribbble.com/shots/25571331-Etail-landing-page-web-design-3D-animation). The artwork is generated for PITCH; source prompts are in `home-art-prompts.txt`. PITCH retains its navy/cyan/white palette, DM Sans and Space Grotesk. The machine wordmark is mounted above its doorway. Hovering or focusing the illustration pauses its animation; reduced-motion preferences show a still line with casual people at the entrance and professionals at the exit.

Practice keeps the scenario, recording and editable response in one wider workspace. Delivery measurements appear only after recording and transcription finish. Feedback appears after evaluation. Text-only responses still work, and saved practice logs retain their existing privacy and deletion behavior.

AI Coach is a separate three-turn roleplay with ten personas, three difficulty levels and three personalities. Guided demo uses clearly labelled scripted replies immediately. Optional WebGPU AI uses the existing Qwen model entirely on-device after an approximately 1 GB initial download. Browser dictation and optional browser read-aloud are separate controls. No paid API, new secrets or user exports are required. Conversations are ephemeral.

Quick play starts immediately with two contestants and an available panel of one or three human judges. Two available judges never produce an even panel. A single-judge match needs one ballot; existing three-judge quorum rules remain in place.

When both contestants opt into the fallback and a compatible pair has waited 30 seconds, one automated rubric judge replaces unavailable human judges. Queue expiry is 30 seconds, with five seconds of grace for the final poll. Automated rounds are explicitly unrated, retain opponent feedback, and do not generate Elo events or judging credits. The stand-in checks submitted text for sentence structure, an example and a next step. These transparent checks are not AI, audio analysis or a skill assessment. Existing peer-only room records remain supported. No database migration is necessary.

Validation: production build and frontend/backend type checks; 57 automated tests cover access boundaries, recording lifecycle, roleplay prompts, practice UI states, three- and five-person matches, the 30-second fallback, privacy and unchanged ratings on automated rounds. Optional on-device model inference was not exercised on a physical GPU as part of this change.
